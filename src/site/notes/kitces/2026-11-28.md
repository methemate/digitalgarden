### The Emotional Empathy Toll of Losing Clients: Kitces & Carl 204
- **Published:** November 26, 2026
- **Category:** Kitces & Carl Podcast
- **Status:** Earlier

Michael Kitces and Carl Richards talk about the grief advisors feel when long-time clients decline and pass away, while they are still expected to steady the family. They suggest sharing specific memories of the client instead of offering generic condolences, and letting grieving families decide whether to tackle tasks now or later. They also stress that advisors need their own ways to process the loss, such as family support or hobbies, since it will happen again and again over a career.

### Adding Tax Preparation In-House Or With External Partners: Strategy, Operations, and Legal/Compliance Issues To Consider
- **Published:** November 25, 2026
- **Category:** Regulation & Compliance
- **Status:** Earlier

Guest authors Russell Kroeger and Chris Stanley compare three ways for advisory firms to bring tax preparation closer to planning: partnering with an outside tax professional, preparing returns in-house, or acquiring a tax firm. Partnering is simplest but gives less control, while in-house prep needs credentialed staff and systems, plus a decision on whether to run it through the RIA or a separate entity. Acquisitions bring talent and cross-sell opportunities, but also culture clashes and change-of-control issues. The authors frame these as points on a cost/control spectrum that firms can move along over time.
